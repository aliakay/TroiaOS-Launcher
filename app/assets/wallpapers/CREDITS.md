## NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day

Downloaded on the TV from the official APOD feed (science.nasa.gov/feed/apod-basic/), never bundled. Many APOD
pictures belong to their photographers, so the app only shows a day whose credit names nothing but public space
agencies and missions (NASA, ESA, JPL, STScI, Hubble, Webb…); on other days it keeps the last free one, or shows
the NASA pictures above. The credit is always on screen.
